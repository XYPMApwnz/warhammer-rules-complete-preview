Your army, reduced to the rules you need

Open a saved guide or paste a New Recruit text export. Death Guard has a personal reader; Adeptus Mechanicus rosters can be saved while its personal reader is still unavailable. The source text and generated record stay in this browser.

What this checks: the exported total against current Army Book points for recognised units, repeat pricing, Enhancements and paid wargear. Missing point data or a mismatch produces a warning and never blocks saving the roster. Roster construction and wargear legality are not checked.
