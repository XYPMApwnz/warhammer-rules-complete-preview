Movement Phase

Move units one at a time, selecting one legal move type for every unit before the phase ends.

09.04

Remain Stationary

PAGE 33
MAXIMUM

–

ELIGIBLE IF

Any unit.

EFFECT

No models move or rotate. The unit does not trigger rules caused by starting or ending a move.

09.05

Normal Move

PAGE 33
MAXIMUM

The unit’s M.

ELIGIBLE IF

On the battlefield and .

EFFECT

Move as described in Moving.

AFTER

The unit must be unengaged.

09.06

Advance Move

PAGE 33
MAXIMUM

M + one D6 Advance roll.

ELIGIBLE IF

On the battlefield and unengaged.

BEFORE

Make an Advance roll.

AFTER

The unit must be unengaged and, unless stated otherwise, cannot declare a charge or start an Action this turn.

09.07

Fall-back Move

PAGE 33
MAXIMUM

The unit’s M.

ELIGIBLE IF

The unit is engaged.

BEFORE

Select Ordered Retreat if not Battle-shocked, or Desperate Escape. Desperate Escape requires one Hazard roll per model.

WHILE

In Desperate Escape, models can move through enemy models.

AFTER

The unit must be unengaged and normally cannot shoot, charge or start an Action this turn. After Desperate Escape, a non-Battle-shocked unit makes a Battle-shock roll.
