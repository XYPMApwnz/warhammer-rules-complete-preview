Core Concepts

The foundational language used throughout every Warhammer 40,000 battle.

Source PDF ↗Next: Attack Sequence →
01.01

Armies

PAGE 8

Each player commands an army made up of units of models. You control all models in your army. The is the player who controls the affected models.

01.02

Units and Models

PAGE 8

A unit contains one or more models. Its models move and fight together as one group, and a model’s base is part of that model for all rules purposes.

  • Friendly units and models are in your army.
  • Enemy units and models are in your opponent’s army.
  • If a rule does not specify friendly or enemy, it affects either.
  • An effect applied to a unit applies to every model in it.
01.03

Active and Opposing Player

PAGE 8

One player is always the ; the other is the opposing player.

  • Outside a player turn, the player taking the first turn that battle round is active.
  • During a move, the moving unit’s controlling player is active until the move ends.
  • During shooting or fighting, the selected unit’s controlling player is active until its attacks are resolved.
01.04

Measuring Distances

PAGE 9

Distances are measured in inches and may be measured at any time. Unless stated otherwise, measure to or from the closest part of a model’s base.

01.05

Dice

PAGE 9
  • 2+: a result of 2 or more.
  • 2D6: roll two D6 and add the results.
  • D3: roll one D6, halve it and round up.
  • D6+1: roll the stated dice, then add the modifier.
01.06

Leadership Rolls

PAGE 9

Roll 2D6. The roll succeeds if the result is equal to or greater than at least one Ld characteristic in that unit; otherwise it fails. The rule requesting the roll states the result.

01.07

Battle-shock Rolls
