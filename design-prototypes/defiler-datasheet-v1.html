Abilities

Core · Deadly Demise D6

Resolve the listed Core ability when this model is destroyed.

Faction · Nurgle’s Gift (Aura)

This model projects the Death Guard faction ability.

Scuttling Walker

When making a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move, this unit can move through models, excluding Titanic models, and terrain features. It can enter Engagement Range during that move but cannot end there. Desperate Escape tests are automatically passed.

Barrage of Filth

After this model has shot, select one enemy unit hit by those attacks. Until the end of the phase, that unit cannot have the Benefit of Cover.

Damaged · 1–6 wounds remaining

Each time this model makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll.

Unit composition
  • 1 Defiler
  • Default: Hades battle cannon, 2 excruciator cannons, heavy missile launcher, heavy baleflamer and shearing claws.
Wargear options
  • Hades battle cannon → ectoplasma destructor.
  • Excruciator cannons → 2 magma cutters.
  • Heavy weapons can be exchanged for a Hades lascannon, heavy reaper autocannon or electroscourge as permitted.
