Stratagems

– Strategic Ploy Stratagem
2CP

WHEN
End of your Movement phase.

TARGET
One unit from your army.

EFFECT
Select one terrain feature within 24" of and visible to your unit. Until the start of your next turn, enemy units are while they are within 3" of that terrain feature.

– Strategic Ploy Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Your Movement phase or your Charge phase.

TARGET
One unit from your army that has not been selected to move or charge this phase.

EFFECT
Until the end of the phase, each time your unit makes a Normal, Advance or Charge move, it can move horizontally through terrain features.

– Battle Tactic Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Your Shooting phase.

TARGET
One unit from your army that has not been selected to shoot this phase.

EFFECT
Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets a visible enemy unit (excluding AIRCRAFT) within your opponent’s deployment zone, you can re-roll the Hit roll.

– Strategic Ploy Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Your opponent’s Command phase.

TARGET
One unit from your army.

EFFECT
Select one objective marker visible to one or more models in your unit. Each enemy unit within range of that objective marker must take a Battle-shock test. Enemy units affected by this Stratagem do not need to take any other Battle-shock tests in the same phase.

– Strategic Ploy Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Start of your opponent’s Charge phase.

TARGET
One unit from your army.

EFFECT
Until the end of the phase, each time an enemy unit selects your unit as the target of a charge, subtract 2 from the Charge roll (this is not cumulative with any other negative modifiers to that Charge roll).
