Explorator Maniple

CODEX + 11E UPDATEPriority Assets2DP

The Quest for Knowledge focuses the army around a chosen objective.

Detachment Rule

Acquisition At Any Cost

At the start of your Command phase, select one objective marker as your Acquisition objective marker until your next Command phase. Each time an ADEPTUS MECHANICUS model attacks, re-roll a Wound roll of 1 if its unit or the target is within range of that marker.

Enhancements

Enhancement

Artisan

While the bearer is leading a unit that is within range of your Acquisition objective marker, once per phase, you can change the result of one Hit roll, one Wound roll or one saving throw made for that unit to an unmodified 6.

Enhancement

Magos

At the end of your Command phase, if the bearer is within range of your Acquisition objective marker, roll one D6: on a 4+, you gain 1CP.

Enhancement

Logis

While the bearer is leading a unit, each time a model in that unit makes an attack that targets a unit within range of your Acquisition objective marker, add 1 to the Hit roll.

Enhancement

Genetor

While the bearer is leading a unit that is within range of your Acquisition objective marker, models in that unit have a 4+ invulnerable save.
