Rad-zone Corps

CODEX + 11E UPDATETake and Hold2DP

The battlefield is saturated with lethal radiation before the assault.

Detachment Rule

Rad-bombardment

At the start of the first battle round, for each enemy unit within your opponent's deployment zone, your opponent chooses whether it takes cover or stands firm. Roll one D6 for each: a unit standing firm suffers D3 mortal wounds on a 3+; a unit taking cover is Battle-shocked and suffers D3 mortal wounds on a 5+. At the start of your Command phase in battle rounds 2-5, roll one D6 for each enemy unit within your opponent's deployment zone; on a 3+, it suffers 1 mortal wound and must take a Battle-shock test.
