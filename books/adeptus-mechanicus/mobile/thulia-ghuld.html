Abilities

This unit has the Faction ability.

In your Command phase, select one ability. Until your next Command phase, this model has that ability.

While this model is within 3" of one or more other friendly ADEPTUS MECHANICUS units, it has .

This model can move through terrain features, but cannot end a move within a wall, a floor, etc. It can be set up or end a move on any floor level of Ruins, but if that level is not the ground floor, it can only do so if its base does not overhang the floor at that level.

At the start of your Shooting phase, select one enemy VEHICLE within 12" and roll one D6; on a 2+, it suffers D3+1 mortal wounds.

Fanatical Devotion lets a friendly SKITARII or THULIA GHULD unit within 6" shoot and charge after Advancing. Adaptive Tactics lets it shoot and charge after Falling Back. The Fires of Mars makes both Imperatives active for it.

THULIA GHULD
An avatar of the Omnissiah’s martial aspect, Thulia Ghuld
commands fanatical devotion from the Martian cohorts she
leads to war. With her Jericho-class conversion resonator,
she blasts swathes of enemies into scattering sonic
energy, while her Rod of the War Forge beams out carrier
waves to goad her warriors into battle.
