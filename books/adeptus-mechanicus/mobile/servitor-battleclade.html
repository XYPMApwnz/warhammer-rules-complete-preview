SERVITOR BATTLECLADE
Servitor Battleclades provide their Adeptus Mechanicus
masters with expendable and extremely versatile combat
assets in the field. Often sent to recover vital archeotech or
data, their mix of firepower, close quarters punch, and array
of servo-tools allows their Underseers to achieve whatever
task is assigned to them.
