Cohort Cybernetica

CODEX + 11E UPDATETake and Hold2DP

Legio Cybernetica engines advance under updated battle-programming.

Detachment Rule

Cyber-psalm Programming

Add 2" to the Move characteristic of models in LEGIO CYBERNETICA units from your army. In addition, unless that unit is Battle-shocked, add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in that unit.

Enhancements

Enhancement

Necromechanic

Once per battle round, when a saving throw made for a friendly Legio Cybernetica or Adeptus Mechanicus Vehicle model within 12" of the bearer is failed, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If they do, change the Damage characteristic of that attack to 0.

Enhancement

Lord of Machines

Once per turn, at the start of your opponent’s Shooting phase, select one enemy Vehicle unit within 12" of and visible to the bearer. That unit must take a Leadership test: if that test is passed, until the end of the phase, each time a model in that unit makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll; if that test is failed, that unit is not eligible to shoot this phase.

Enhancement

Emotionless Clarity

Once per turn, when a friendly LEGIO CYBERNETICA or ADEPTUS MECHANICUS VEHICLE model with the Deadly Demise ability that is within 12" of the bearer is destroyed, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, do not roll to determine whether any mortal wounds are inflicted as a result of that model’s Deadly Demise ability. Instead, mortal wounds are automatically inflicted.

Enhancement

Arch-negator
