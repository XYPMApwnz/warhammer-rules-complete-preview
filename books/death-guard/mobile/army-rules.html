Core Stratagems

Universal Stratagems available to every army. Datasheet tools only show the cards the selected unit can legally target.

COMMAND RE-ROLL

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Any phase, just after you make an Advance, Charge, Damage, Hazard, Hit, Save or Wound roll for a unit from your army, or a roll to determine the number of attacks made with a weapon equipped by a model in that unit.

TARGET
That unit or model from your army.

EFFECT
Re-roll that roll. If several dice were rolled, select one of them to re-roll; Charge rolls are re-rolled in full.

EPIC CHALLENGE

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Fight phase, just after a friendly CHARACTER unit is selected to fight.

TARGET
That CHARACTER unit.

EFFECT
Select one CHARACTER model in that unit. Until the end of the phase, its melee weapons have the PRECISION ability.

INSANE BRAVERY

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Battle-shock step of your Command phase, just before a unit from your army takes a Battle-shock test.

TARGET
That unit.

EFFECT
That test is automatically successful.

RESTRICTION
Once per battle.

EXPLOSIVES

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Your Shooting phase.

TARGET
One EXPLOSIVES or GRENADES unit from your army that is eligible to shoot, did not Advance this turn and is not within Engagement Range of enemy units.

EFFECT
Select one eligible model, then one visible enemy unit within 8" and outside Engagement Range. Roll six D6: for each 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.

CRUSHING IMPACT

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Your Charge phase, just after a MONSTER or VEHICLE unit from your army ends a Charge move.

TARGET
That MONSTER or VEHICLE unit.

EFFECT
Select one enemy unit within Engagement Range and one model in your unit within Engagement Range of it. Roll a number of D6 equal to that model’s Toughness: each 1 inflicts 1 mortal wound on your unit; each 5+ inflicts 1 mortal wound on the enemy unit, to a maximum of 6.

RAPID INGRESS

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
End of your opponent’s Movement phase.

TARGET
One unit from your army in Strategic Reserves, excluding AIRCRAFT.

EFFECT
That unit can make an ingress move.

RESTRICTION
Cannot be used during the first battle round.

FIRE OVERWATCH

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
End of your opponent’s Movement phase.

TARGET
One unit from your army, excluding TITANIC units, that is not within Engagement Range of enemy units.

EFFECT
Your unit can shoot one visible enemy unit within 24" using Snap Shooting: only unmodified Hit rolls of 6 score a hit and Hit rolls cannot be re-rolled. After it has shot, that unit is not eligible to perform an Action this turn.

SMOKESCREEN

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Start of your opponent’s Shooting phase.

TARGET
One SMOKE unit from your army.

EFFECT
Until the end of the phase, that unit has the Benefit of Cover. In addition, a friendly unit not fully visible to the attacking unit because it is obscured by the selected SMOKE unit also has the Benefit of Cover against that attack.

HEROIC INTERVENTION

Core Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
End of your opponent’s Charge phase.

TARGET
One unit from your army within 12" of one or more enemy units and not within Engagement Range. A VEHICLE can only be targeted if it is also a CHARACTER or WALKER.

EFFECT
Resolve a Charge and select one mode before the Charge roll. Leap to Defend: charge targets must have made a Charge move this phase and be within the maximum distance. Into the Fray (+1CP): if the modified Charge roll is greater than 6, change it to 6; charge targets can be any enemy units within 6" and within the maximum distance.

COUNTEROFFENSIVE

Core Stratagem
2CP

WHEN
Your opponent’s Fight step, just after an enemy unit has resolved its attacks.

TARGET
One unit from your army that is eligible to fight.

EFFECT
Your unit has Fights First and must be the next unit selected to fight.
