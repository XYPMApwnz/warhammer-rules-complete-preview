Stratagems

– Battle Tactic Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

TARGET
One from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

EFFECT
Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ scores a Critical Hit.

– Battle Tactic Stratagem
2CP

WHEN
Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase.

TARGET
One from your army that has not been selected to shoot or fight this phase.

EFFECT
Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack that targets a unit that is below its Starting Strength, you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll.

– Battle Tactic Stratagem
1CP

WHEN
Your opponent’s Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

TARGET
One from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.

EFFECT
Until the end of the phase, add 2 to the Toughness characteristic of models in your unit.
