Other Rules and Abilities

Aura, Faction, Psychic and Wargear abilities.

11E reference ↗← Flying and SurgingAircraft →

rule

Abilities that affect models or units a stated range are , and are tagged with the word ‘Aura’.

While a model with an aura ability is on the battlefield, it is always range of its own aura ability.

A unit can be affected by more than one aura ability at a time, but if a unit is range of the same aura ability more than once, that aura ability only applies to that unit once.

rule

Some abilities are common to each unit that belongs to a particular faction – these are (also known as army rules), and are listed in the section of a datasheet.

Unless otherwise stated, a unit’s only apply if the army faction selected while mustering your army matches a faction keyword listed on that unit’s datasheet.

rule

Abilities tagged with the word ‘Psychic’ are . If a psychic ability causes a model to lose one or more wounds, each of those wounds is said to be inflicted by a psychic attack (this can be important for the triggering of other rules).
