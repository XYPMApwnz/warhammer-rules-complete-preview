Placing Units in Strategic Reserves

rule

Before the battle, in the , you can select one or more friendly units (excluding ) to place in . Instead of setting up these units on the battlefield during , place them to one side; they are strategic reserves units, and will arrive later in the battle.

Unless otherwise stated, the combined points value of all of your strategic reserves units (including those embarked within that are themselves placed in strategic reserves) cannot exceed 50% of your points limit for your battle size.

Strategic Reserves at the End of the Battle

At the end of the final turn, units in are destroyed, but they do not trigger rules that apply when a model is destroyed.

Repositioned Units

rule

Some rules allow units to be removed from the battlefield and placed in during the battle. Units that use such rules are known as repositioned units. In addition to any other rules that apply to such units (such as where they can or cannot arrive), all of the following rules apply to them:

  • If used in the , such rules can be used on units that have already moved that phase.
  • A repositioned unit that is set up in the same turn in which it made an advance, fall-back or has still made an advance, fall-back or disembark move that turn.
  • When they are removed from the battlefield, any rules that are affecting such units for a specified duration or under specified circumstances continue to affect them while that duration and/or those circumstances apply.

Example: A unit that was within range of an aura ability when removed from the battlefield would no longer be affected by that aura ability if it is no longer within range of it when it makes an , but a unit that was when removed from the battlefield would still be battle-shocked if it makes an ingress move in the same turn.
