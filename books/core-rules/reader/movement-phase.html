Selecting Units To Move
Digital 11E

When the ends, the must have selected all of their units to make a move with, including those in strategic . This means that before the phase ends, every unit in their army will have been selected to make a move, even if it is to . Any units the active player does not select a move type for during this step will remain stationary.

Selecting Modes
Digital 11E

Some rules instruct you to select a mode, such as fall-back moves (). Modes are mutually exclusive, and you must assess each one in the order presented. When making a move, if your unit does not meet the conditions of any of the modes, it cannot make that move.

Sometimes a mode will be mandatory if applicable (e.g. consolidation modes, , but in the case of fall-back moves, ordered retreat is not mandatory, so you could select instead.

Many move types state conditions you must meet while/after moving. Those that are labelled with a mode name only apply if you selected that mode; those not labelled with a mode name always apply.

Desperate Escape Test
Digital 11E

Some rules may reference the term 'Desperate Escape test'. In all instances this means the made for the mode of a .

Abilities that subtract from a unit's Desperate Escape test instead subtract from those hazard rolls.

Example: A battle-shocked unit makes a fall-back move away from a unit of Bloodletters that have the Bane of Cowards ability. As a result of Bane of Cowards, because the unit making the fall-back move is battle-shocked, the hazard rolls they make as part of their desperate escape will be at -1.
