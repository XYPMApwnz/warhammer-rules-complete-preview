Shooting

Charging and Fighting

Official PDF · page 74
  • AIRCRAFT units are not eligible to declare a charge, and can only make melee attacks that target FLYING units.
  • Only FLYING units can select AIRCRAFT units as a charge target, and only can make melee attacks that target AIRCRAFT units.
