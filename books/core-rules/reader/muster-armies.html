Muster Armies

Army faction, battle size, Detachment Points, unit limits, Warlord and Enhancements.

11E reference ↗← Core Abilities

muster

The details of your army must be recorded on an army roster. can either write this down or use the Warhammer 40,000 app. Players should show their finished army roster to their opponent before battle commences.

muster

Note on your army roster one faction keyword to be your army faction.

muster

With your opponent, select one of the battle sizes below. This will determine the total number of points (also known as the points total) each player can spend to build their army. The points values for units can be found in the Warhammer 40,000 app. will need to refer to these when building your army. The battle size will also determine the total number of Points (DP) players will have to unlock detachments with, the number of units with the same that they can include in their army, and the number of enhancements that they can include in their army. These are summarised below.

will now select your detachments, units, and enhancements that will be in your army. will also attach your and units to other units.

When doing so:

  • cannot exceed any of the values presented in the table (). For example, in an Incursion battle, cannot select detachments with a combined value of more than 2 DP.
  • No unit (including attached units) can have more than one enhancement.
  • Your army roster must follow all restrictions placed on it by the rules and units being included in it.
