Muster Armies

Army faction, battle size, Detachment Points, unit limits, Warlord and Enhancements.

Official GW PDF ↗← Core Abilities

Start Your Army Roster

Digital 11E

The details of your army must be recorded on an army roster. You can either write this down or use the Warhammer 40,000 app. Players should show their finished army roster to their opponent before battle commences.

Select Army Faction

Digital 11E

Note on your army roster one faction keyword to be your army faction.

Select Battle Size

Digital 11E

With your opponent, select one of the battle sizes below. This will determine the total number of points (also known as the points total) each player can spend to build their army. The points values for units can be found in the Warhammer 40,000 app. You will need to refer to these when building your army. The battle size will also determine the total number of Points (DP) players will have to unlock detachments with, the number of units with the same that they can include in their army, and the number of enhancements that they can include in their army. These are summarised below.

* The unit limit for and units is double the relevant amount shown above, and every has a unit limit of 1, regardless of the battle size.

Battle sizePointsDPEnhancementsUnit limit
Incursion1000222
Strike Force2000343

Fill Your Army Roster

Digital 11E

You will now select your detachments, units, and enhancements that will be in your army. You will also attach your and units to other units.

When doing so:

  • You cannot exceed any of the values presented in the table. For example, in an Incursion battle, you cannot select detachments with a combined value of more than 2 DP.
  • No unit (including attached units) can have more than one enhancement.
  • Your army roster must follow all restrictions placed on it by the rules and units being included in it.
